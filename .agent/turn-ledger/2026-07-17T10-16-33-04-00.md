## Intent

Define one authority that makes declared stage lights, custom-material shading, shadow-map work and the visible frame describe the same accepted lighting generation.

## Findings

- `renderer.shadowMap.enabled` is true.
- The directional light casts shadows and props cast/receive shadows.
- A hemisphere light is present.
- All visible authored surfaces use custom `ShaderMaterial` instances.
- The custom shader uses a fixed `lightDir` uniform.
- The shader consumes neither Three.js scene lights nor shadow maps.
- Scene-light changes therefore do not control visible shading.
- Shadow work can be admitted without a material receiver that samples it.
